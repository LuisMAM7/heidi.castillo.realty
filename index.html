The Trust Factor

Legal Security & Risk Mitigation

Buying real estate in Venezuela as an expatriate or international investor is safe when backed by a structured, verified 4-step acquisition roadmap.

01

Initial Screening & Due Diligence

Verification of seller legitimacy, property identity, encumbrances, and municipal tax payment history.

02

Chain-of-Title Audit

Deep historical review through public registries to guarantee zero hidden liens or ownership disputes.

03

Purchase Contract Drafting

Bilingual escrow conditions, clear payment terms, and Power of Attorney handling if you are abroad.

04

Registry Closing & Deed Handover

Official registration before the Venezuelan Public Registry (SAREN) and physical delivery of registered title deeds.
